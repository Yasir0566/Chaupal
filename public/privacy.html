Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Chaupal is a live polling application. This policy explains how Chaupal handles information.

Information We Collect

If you use Google Sign-In, Chaupal receives your name, email address, profile picture, and Google account identifier. Guest users receive a randomly generated name and avatar.

Chaupal also stores polls, poll options, votes, and account information needed to operate the service.

How We Use Information

We use information to sign users in, create and display polls, record votes, prevent duplicate votes, and maintain the application.

Google User Data

Google account information is used only for authentication and identifying your Chaupal account. Chaupal does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising.

Service Providers

Hosting and database providers may process information as necessary to operate Chaupal.

Data Deletion

To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at diresow@gmail.com.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to diresow@gmail.com.
